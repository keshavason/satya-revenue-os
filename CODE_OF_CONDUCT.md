# Code of Conduct

Contributors must engage respectfully, critique claims and code rather than
people, protect private information, attribute work, and make room for good-faith
objection. Harassment, discrimination, deceptive evidence, retaliation, and
coercive commercial conduct are not accepted.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's
documented security/contact channel once one is established. Until then, do not
include personal or sensitive details in public issues.
