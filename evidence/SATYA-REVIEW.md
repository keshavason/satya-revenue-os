   outreach, money movement, live CRM mutation, or fabricated performance claims.
   The candidate stays within that mandate.
2. **Creation:** original Node.js implementation with zero runtime dependencies;
   sources informed patterns but no external corpus or source code was copied.
3. **Foundation:** claims, evidence classes, formulas, policy decisions, approvals,
   receipts, and limitations are machine-visible and versioned. Evals are labeled
   fixture-conformance rather than live proof.
4. **Criticism:** independent reviews found one high, multiple medium, and low
   issues during construction. Negative results and their corrections are retained
   in `CORRECTIONS.md`; the final scan still reports two low limitations.
5. **Deliberation:** framework neutrality was retained because no researched
   orchestration framework replaces domain contracts, effect idempotency, or
   evidence semantics. Durable workflow engines remain optional adapters.
6. **Correction:** path traversal, executable skill loading, canonical collisions,
   tenant/idempotency gaps, mutable state, unbounded recursion, provenance trust,
   package closure, and release-inventory gaps were corrected and retested.
7. **Conservation:** `satya-review` was not modified; no remote, credentials, live
   connector, publication, or external service effect was created. Personal or
   client data was not imported.
8. **Learning:** accepted limits are converted into explicit P1 gates: durable
   state, authenticated audit/provenance, live-behavior evaluation, and human
   publication authorization. No automated review is labeled human approval.
